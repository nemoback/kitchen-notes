instructions:
  - "Peel the onion and garlic. Finely dice the onion; crush and chop the garlic."
  - "Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan. Cook the onion and garlic over medium heat until lightly golden."
  - "Add the mince. Fry for 10 minutes, stirring and breaking up any lumps."
  - "Remove the peppers’ seeds and membranes. Dice the flesh and cook with the meat for another 5 minutes."
  - "Peel and blend the tomatoes. Add to the pan with salt, pepper, and herbs. Simmer gently for 20 minutes."
  - "Bring salted water to a boil. Lower in whole spaghetti gradually as it softens. Cook for about 10 minutes, checking the packet instructions."
  - "Drain without rinsing. Add a little olive oil if desired."
  - "Toss the spaghetti with the sauce and heat through. Finish with grated cheese and serve immediately."
