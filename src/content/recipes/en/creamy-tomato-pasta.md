  - { amount: 3, unit: "cloves", item: "garlic, thinly sliced" }
  - { amount: 400, unit: "g", item: "cherry tomatoes, halved" }
  - { amount: 120, unit: "ml", item: "heavy cream" }
  - { amount: 35, unit: "g", item: "parmesan, finely grated" }
  - { amount: 1, unit: "handful", item: "fresh basil leaves" }
  - { item: "Sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper" }
instructions:
  - "Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta until just shy of al dente, reserving a mug of pasta water before draining."
  - "Warm the olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds, then add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt. Let them soften and burst for 8–10 minutes."
  - "Stir in the cream and half the parmesan. Add the drained pasta and toss, loosening the sauce with a splash of pasta water until glossy."
  - "Finish with basil, the remaining parmesan, and plenty of black pepper."

The kind of supper that turns an ordinary Tuesday into something to look forward to. Keep a little pasta water handy: it brings the sauce together beautifully.
