  - { amount: 2, unit: "tbsp", item: "20% sour cream" }
  - { amount: 1.5, unit: "glasses", item: "wheat flour" }
  - { amount: 1, unit: "tsp", item: "baking powder" }
  - { amount: 400, unit: "g", item: "apples" }
  - { amount: 150, unit: "g", item: "sugar — filling" }
  - { amount: 40, unit: "ml", item: "vegetable oil" }
instructions:
  - "Core and chop the apples; peeling is optional."
  - "Warm the oil. Cover and gently soften the apples: 5–8 minutes for summer and autumn varieties, 10–15 for winter varieties. Do not reduce to a purée."
  - "Remove from heat and stir in the filling sugar."
  - "Cream the butter with the dough sugar, then add sour cream."
  - "Sift flour, combine with baking powder, and add to the butter mixture."
  - "Briefly knead a soft dough; avoid prolonged kneading."
  - "Line a 20 cm tin with parchment. For a fixed-base tin, place two paper strips beneath the liner to lift out the pie."
  - "Preheat to 180°C. Reserve one-third of the dough; roll the rest and line the base and sides."
  - "Spread the apple filling inside."
  - "Roll the remaining dough 5 mm thick and cut 1 cm strips. Form a lattice and press the edges with a fork."
  - "Bake 35–40 minutes until golden, adjusting for your oven."
  - "Serve warm or cooled."
